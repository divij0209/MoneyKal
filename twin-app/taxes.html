      What it does

      Your return, computed from your own numbers

      Every figure is calculated from the income you enter and the rules for the assessment year, not estimated from a rule of thumb.

      • Income heads

        All five heads, collected properly

        Salary, house property, business and profession, capital gains, and other sources. Business income can be declared presumptively or under normal provisions, and capital gains are separated by holding period and asset class.

      • Salary detail

        HRA and perquisites worked out in full

        House rent allowance is computed against the statutory three way test, with metro status resolved from your city. Motor car perquisites are valued by engine capacity and usage, and each statutory ceiling is noted when it actually bites.

      • Deductions

        Limits applied, not assumed

        Chapter VI A deductions and TDS already paid are collected and capped at their real limits. Where a deduction exists only under the old regime, it is reported rather than silently netted off the new one.

      • Computation

        Slabs, surcharge, cess and marginal relief

        Tax is built band by band, then surcharge is applied at the right threshold, marginal relief is granted where it applies, and cess is added last. The slab breakdown is shown so the total can be traced.

      • Regime comparison

        Old against new, on your figures

        Both regimes are computed in full and set side by side. Where the old regime loses, MoneyKal calculates how much additional deduction would be needed to break even, so the choice is a number rather than a guess.

      • Filing

        Recommendations and the right ITR form

        You get specific actions based on what your return actually shows, and a recommended ITR form with the reason each other form was ruled out.

      How it works

      Six steps, in the order a return is built

      The flow follows the computation itself, so nothing is asked for before it is needed.

      1. 01

        Income heads

        Enter what you earned, head by head. Only the heads that apply to you are asked for.

      2. 02

        HRA exemption

        Rent, basic pay and city decide the exempt portion under the statutory test.

      3. 03

        Deductions and TDS

        Chapter VI A deductions are capped at their limits, and tax already deducted is credited.

      4. 04

        Computation and regime comparison

        Both regimes are computed through slabs, surcharge, marginal relief and cess, then compared.

      5. 05

        Recommendations

        Specific actions drawn from what your own computation shows.

      6. 06

        ITR form selection

        The form that fits your return, with the reason each alternative was ruled out.
