                    Example — not your data

                    “Should I buy a car worth 12 lakh with a 5 year loan at 9.5%?”

                    Monthly EMI ₹25,202
                    Surplus after ₹42,798/mo
                    Total interest ₹3,12,134
                    Verdict Affordable

                    How it is calculated EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), on a reducing balance, with P = ₹12,00,000, r = 9.5% ÷ 12 and n = 60.

                    • Assumes the full price is financed with no down payment.
                    • Excludes insurance, registration and running costs.
                    • Every figure is computed here, not written by a language model.

                    What MoneyKal holds, and what it will never ask for.

                    MoneyKal PIN

                    Checking…

                    A 4- or 6-digit PIN unlocks MoneyKal on a device you chose to stay signed in on. It is checked on our servers and never stored on your device. Your password is still required every 3 days.

                    • Your password is stored as a bcrypt hash. Nobody, including us, can read it back.
                    • No payment credentials. There is no field anywhere in MoneyKal for a card number, UPI PIN or net-banking login.
                    • Your data is yours alone. Every figure is locked to this account; no other account can read it.
                    • AI answers send a pseudonymous copy of your figures to a model provider — never your name, email or account id.
