Varta

Speak. Ask. Understand. Varta is the voice interface to MoneyKal. Ask the way you would think it, and the answer comes back from your own numbers.

What it does

A conversation with your own figures

Varta is the same intelligence the rest of MoneyKal uses, reached by speaking instead of typing.

  • Interaction

    Talk, and interrupt

    Varta runs as a live voice session in the browser. You speak naturally and can cut in while it is talking, the way you would in a real conversation, rather than waiting for a turn to finish.

  • Grounding

    Answers from your position

    Varta answers from your own financial data rather than from general knowledge, and the figure behind an answer is shown alongside it so you can check the reasoning instead of trusting it.

  • Continuity

    It remembers the thread

    Turns within a session are kept, so a follow up question does not need you to restate what you were asking about.

  • Transcripts

    Shown live, not stored by default

    What you say is transcribed on screen as you speak. Those transcripts are not written to your chat history or the database unless persistence is deliberately switched on.

How it works

One session, start to finish

The flow is designed so a spoken answer is as checkable as a written one.

  1. 01

    Open a session

    MoneyKal issues a short lived session token scoped to you and the profile you are working in.

  2. 02

    Ask in plain speech

    Speech is transcribed live, and you can interrupt the assistant at any point.

  3. 03

    It fetches real figures

    When answering needs your data, the assistant calls back into MoneyKal for the specific numbers rather than inventing them.

  4. 04

    You hear the answer and see the basis

    The spoken answer arrives with the figure it used displayed next to it.

Worth knowing

Availability

The honest boundary of the feature.

Voice needs to be switched on for the deployment

Varta depends on a configured voice provider and a publicly reachable callback, so it is available when the deployment has been set up for it. Where voice is not configured, the rest of MoneyKal is unaffected and the same questions can be asked in writing. Varta runs in the browser, and on a deployment with telephony configured it can also call the phone number saved on your profile and talk you through your numbers there.
