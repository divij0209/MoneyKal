What the layer does

From information to decision

The intelligence is in the arithmetic and the checks around it, not in confident language.

  • Computation

    Figures are computed, not produced

    Position, cash flow, buffer months, runway and financial health are calculated from your ledger and profile. A language model may phrase an answer, but it does not decide the numbers in it.

  • Pipeline

    A staged, visible process

    A question moves through named stages: your data is retrieved, a recommendation is computed, the inputs are validated for risk, and the underlying concept is explained. Each stage reports what it did, so an answer arrives with its own audit trail.

  • Simulation

    Run the decision before you make it

    Describe a decision and MoneyKal projects it forward against your real figures, showing the effect on savings, goal timeline, monthly cash flow and buffer together rather than one at a time.

  • Scoring

    A health score with a breakdown

    Financial health is scored out of a hundred with the components that produced it shown, so the number can be argued with rather than simply accepted.

  • Context

    Market signal filtered to you

    Market Pulse scores headlines against what you actually hold and what you are saving toward, then keeps the few that change something for your position.

  • Guardrails

    It declines rather than guesses

    Where the data does not support a conclusion, MoneyKal says so and names what is missing instead of returning a confident number built on defaults.
