Tathya

Ask. Analyze. Act. Tathya works over your own figures and surfaces the one thing worth knowing today, with the basis it used shown next to it.

What it analyses

Readings computed from your ledger

Each reading is produced by a rule that inspects your real data and either returns a result or returns nothing, because the data did not support one.

  • Comparison

    This week against your usual week

    Your spending over the last seven days is measured against your own established pattern rather than an external benchmark, so the comparison means something for your life rather than an average one.

  • Movement

    This month against last

    Month over month movement is computed per category, which is what turns a vague sense that things feel expensive into a specific category and a specific figure.

  • Concentration

    The category that actually moved

    A spike is ranked by how much it moves your position, not by how large it looks in isolation, so a small category doubling does not outrank the one that actually changed your month.

  • Pressure

    What is due against what you have

    Obligations falling in the next thirty days are weighed against the money genuinely available once goals and buffer are accounted for.

  • Pace

    Whether a goal is on time

    Goal progress is checked against the date you set, so a target slipping is surfaced while there is still time to change it.

  • Resilience

    Whether the buffer is thin

    Your emergency buffer is expressed in months of expenses covered, and flagged when it drops below the level where a shock would force a bad decision.

How it decides

Why the readings are checkable

The distinction that matters most in financial software is whether a number was computed or produced.

Rules, not a language model guessing

Every reading Tathya produces is computed deterministically and marked as generated by rules. A rule that cannot find support in your data returns nothing rather than softening its language, which is why you will sometimes see fewer readings rather than weaker ones.

How it works

From ledger to reading

The path from your data to what you read is short and inspectable.

  1. 01

    It derives the shared picture

    Weekly and monthly bounds, category totals and money in against money out are computed once from your ledger.

  2. 02

    Each rule inspects it

    Every rule looks at that picture and decides whether your data genuinely supports its reading.

  3. 03

    Results are ranked

    Surviving readings are ordered by how much they actually move your position.

  4. 04

    One reading is surfaced

    The most useful reading your data supports is shown, with the figures it was computed from.
