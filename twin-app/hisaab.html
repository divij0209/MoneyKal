    Where it went

      Illustrative month. In the product these are your own entries.

      What it does

      The ledger everything else reads from

      Hisaab is the source of truth. Insights, simulations and the voice answers all compute over these entries, which is why the categories are fixed and every figure is traceable to a row.

      • Capture

        Scan a receipt

        Photograph a receipt and MoneyKal reads it, pulling out the merchant, the date, the line items, the tax amount and the total, then proposes a category. You confirm or correct before anything is saved.

      • Ledger

        Money in and money out

        Every entry is income or expense, with an amount, a date, a description and a category. Add, edit and delete entries directly; the totals and the net figure recompute from the ledger rather than being stored separately.

      • Structure

        One fixed category set

        Twenty five categories cover income and expenses, from Salary, Freelance and Investment Return through Rent, Groceries, Utilities, Subscriptions, Payroll, Software, Marketing and Taxes. A fixed list is what makes month against month comparison meaningful.

      • Automation

        Import from Gmail

        Connect Gmail and MoneyKal reads bank transaction alerts, turning them into entries you review before they land. It also spots payment deadlines in the same mail and offers them to your calendar.

      How it works

      Four steps, no bookkeeping knowledge needed

      The flow is deliberately short. The work is in the reading, not the data entry.

      1. 01

        Add what happened

        Scan a receipt, log an entry by hand, or let a connected Gmail account propose entries from bank alerts.

      2. 02

        Confirm the reading

        Nothing is saved from a scan or an import until you accept it. You can change the category, the amount or the date first.

      3. 03

        Watch the totals move

        Money in, money out and the net figure recompute against the ledger, and the category breakdown updates with them.

      4. 04

        Read it back

        Weekly and monthly summaries compare this period against your usual one, and feed the readings you see elsewhere in MoneyKal.

      Worth knowing

      Where the numbers come from

      MoneyKal is explicit about which figures are computed and which are read from a document.

      Receipt scanning uses a vision model

      Extraction is a language model reading an image, so it can misread a smudged or unusual receipt. That is exactly why the proposed values are shown for confirmation rather than written straight into your ledger.
