Security & Privacy

Nothing we hold can be used to take your money

We hold the least information that still lets us give you a useful answer, every piece of it is locked to your account alone, and nothing we hold can be used to take your money.

  • Your money

    We never hold it or move it

    No payment integration, no mandate, no instruction we can send to a bank. Even in the worst compromise of our systems, nobody gains the ability to move a rupee through us.

  • Credentials

    We never ask for them

    We do not ask for your net-banking password, card number or CVV, and there is nowhere in our system to put them even if you offered.

  • Connections

    Permission to read, never to act

    Anything you connect is read-only, and you can withdraw the permission from your own account settings without involving us.

Mechanisms

How your account is protected

Each of these is implemented today.

  • Passwords

    Stored one way, never readable

    Put through bcrypt with a random value unique to your account. We store only the result, so neither we nor anyone holding a copy of our database can work back to your password.

  • Sessions

    A signed pass, checked every request

    Identity is proved by signature rather than by claim, so a forged pass is rejected before any data is read. The pass that keeps you signed in cannot be used to do anything else, and on phones it lives in the device keychain behind your screen lock.

  • Isolation

    Locked to your account

    Our servers work out whose account it is from the verified pass, then restrict every database lookup to that owner. A request for someone else’s record returns nothing at all, not an error confirming it exists.

  • In transit

    HTTPS, and no pass in a web address

    Addresses end up in browser history and server logs, so your session pass never goes into one. Connections to outside services use a separate ticket that expires in two minutes and can do only the one thing it was created for.

  • The assistant

    One account’s context at a time

    The AI has no database access of its own and reaches information through a single door that checks authorisation on every request. It cannot talk its way into another account, because what it claims is not what we check.

Where we stand

We are not a bank and not regulated by the RBI, and we are not a registered investment adviser — MoneyKal models scenarios in your own numbers, it does not tell you what to buy. We are a Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act, 2023, and building to its requirements.

Incidents

If something goes wrong

What we can do immediately, what we will tell you, and the part of this we are weakest at.

  • Containment

    Every session, invalidated at once

    We can invalidate every active session across the entire platform immediately. It signs everybody out, which is disruptive, and that is the point — our first response to a suspected compromise does not have to wait for us to understand it fully.

  • Notification

    You hear it from us

    After containment we work out what was reached and whose data was involved. If your data was affected we will tell you what happened, what it means for you and what to do about it — and we will tell you even when the news is embarrassing for us. Under the DPDP Act we also notify the Data Protection Board, and a qualifying incident is reported to CERT-In within six hours.

  • Detection

    The weakest link, and we know it

    Improving our ability to detect a problem in the first place, rather than being told about one, is an active piece of work. It is honest to say that a small pre-launch team’s detection capability is the weakest link in any incident plan, including ours.

Found a security problem?

Please tell us before you tell anyone else, and we will not threaten you for it. Write to us with enough detail to reproduce the issue. We will acknowledge you within three working days, keep you updated, and credit you when it is fixed if you would like us to.

Data

What we collect, and what we never will

Every field on the left exists because a feature uses it; if something stopped driving an answer we would stop collecting it. There is no field anywhere in MoneyKal for anything on the right, so that is not a policy we could drift away from — the place to store it does not exist.

What we collect
  • Your email address and a one-way hash of your password
  • Income, expenses, savings, loans, debt and the goals you set
  • Transaction amounts, categories, dates and descriptions
  • Bills, EMIs, card dues and subscriptions, with their due dates
  • Business details such as stage, headcount and burn, for startup accounts only
  • Your conversations with the assistant, and the reasoning behind each answer
  • A WhatsApp number, only if you choose to add one
  • A read-only permission for Gmail or accounting software, if you connect one
What we never collect
  • Net-banking usernames or passwords
  • Debit or credit card numbers, expiry dates or CVVs
  • UPI PINs or any payment credential
  • Aadhaar numbers
  • PAN details
  • Bank account numbers as a required field
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Your location or your contacts list

Artificial intelligence

What leaves MoneyKal when the AI answers

Every number in the product is calculated here, on our own servers, by code you can read the working of. Language models are used to phrase an answer around figures that were already computed — never to produce a figure. That still means something leaves, and this is exactly what.

What is sent to a model provider
  • A pseudonymous snapshot of your figures: income, expenses, savings, loans, monthly surplus, buffer in months, and a goal’s title, target and progress
  • The question you typed, and the recent turns of that conversation
  • For a startup account, the same for revenue, burn, runway and headcount
  • If you use voice, the audio clip of what you said, for transcription only
What is never sent
  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your password, in any form
  • Your account identifier, or anything that maps back to you
  • Your individual transactions, bills or Hisaab ledger rows
  • Any connected mailbox’s contents
  • Recipients

    Google and Groq, from our server only

    Google (Gemini) phrases simulation recommendations, reads uploaded statements and summarises market news. Groq phrases the assistant’s replies and turns voice into text. Both are reached over their commercial APIs from our backend — your browser never contacts them, and neither is given a way to identify you.

  • Precision

    Pseudonymous, which is not the same as anonymous

    Nothing we send names you. We are not claiming the figures themselves could never be recognised by someone who already knew a great deal about your finances. That is a real distinction and we would rather state it than blur it.

  • Limits

    What happens next is not ours to promise

    Once a request reaches a provider, retention and handling are governed by that provider’s terms rather than ours. We do not hold an agreement with either that excludes your data from their standard API handling, and we do not claim one.

  • Opting out

    Everything else sends nothing outward

    The AI features are the assistant, simulation wording, statement import and market summaries. Overview, Hisaab, Money Splits, the tax engine, goals and reports are all computed on our own servers. If you do not use the assistant, nothing of yours reaches a model provider at all.

Your control

It is your data, and you can take it back

You are lending it to us so we can be useful to you, and lending implies you can end it.

  • Correct

    Everything is editable

    Anything you have entered can be reviewed and changed in the app at any time.

  • Disconnect

    You can cut us off without asking

    Disconnect Gmail or your accounting software from your dashboard, or revoke us directly from your Google account settings. Your ability to cut us off does not depend on us behaving well.

  • Delete

    Manual today, self-serve in build

    Deleting your account removes your profile, transactions, bills, goals, conversation history and any connection permissions. Today you ask us and we do it by hand; a delete button and a machine-readable export are both in the current build.

  • Retention

    Until you ask us to remove it

    Your data stays until you ask. A published retention schedule is part of the same piece of work — data we no longer hold cannot leak, so it is a security measure too.

Who can see your data

Nobody here can see your password; it is one-way hashed, so the information is not there to look at. Engineers currently have the database access that building the product requires. Least-privilege roles and access logging come with the move to managed infrastructure, and until then what protects you is a small team and a clear rule, not a system.
