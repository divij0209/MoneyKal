What you can do

The product, in your pocket

The app is a full client rather than a companion. It reads and writes the same data as the web.

  • Daily

    Home and Hisaab

    Your position, readings and calendar on opening, and the full ledger for logging an expense or scanning a receipt the moment it happens rather than remembering it later.

  • Ask

    Questions and voice

    Ask a question in writing or open Varta and speak it. Answers come from the same computed figures the web uses.

  • Decide

    Simulate and reports

    Run a decision forward on the phone and read the weekly and monthly reports without waiting to get back to a desk.

  • Watch

    Alerts and Market Pulse

    Due dates, unusual spending and goal pace surface as alerts, with market context filtered to what you actually hold.

  • For founders

    A startup view

    Accounts set up as a startup get their own tab set built around cash, runway and burn instead of personal spending.

  • Protection

    A lock on the app itself

    Set a passcode, and use your device biometrics to unlock. Credentials are held in the device keychain rather than ordinary app storage.

How it is built

One backend, two clients

Worth knowing where the app is in its life.

Built with React Native and Expo

The app targets iOS and Android from one codebase and talks to the same MoneyKal API as the website, so a change made on either side is immediately true on the other. It is not yet published on the app stores; it currently runs as a development build.
