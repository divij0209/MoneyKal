How we think about it

Why this is the line we drew

  • The free plan

    Not a demo of the real thing

    The tax engine, the simulation engine and the assistant are the product, and they are all on the free plan with no usage limit. A person trying to work out whether they can afford something should not meet a paywall at the moment they ask.

  • What you pay for

    Work with a marginal cost

    Receipt scanning, split insights and statement import each cost us money on every run. Charging for those, rather than for the core, is what keeps the core free rather than eventually rationed.

  • Splitting

    Free for everyone you split with

    Creating groups, adding expenses, settling up and being invited are free on both sides, permanently. Someone you owe money to never needs an account, let alone a paid one, for you to settle with them.

  • What we do not do

    Your data is not the product

    No advertising, no selling or brokering your information, and no commission from any lender, fund or insurer for a recommendation. Subscriptions are the only revenue, which is the only arrangement under which advice can be neutral.
